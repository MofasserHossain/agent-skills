# Continuation record

## Objective and scope
The requested outcome and current work boundary.

## Where to resume
Project/workspace location, repository, branch/revision and authoritative task/spec pointers. State anything that must be transferred or reprovisioned.

## Settled decisions
Consequential choices and reasons, with references to their source.

## Current work and evidence
Completed versus claimed outcomes; modified/untracked files and local-only commits; checks actually run and their revision. Link evidence rather than pasting logs.

## Remaining work and blockers
Unmet criteria, unanswered decisions, environment/access gaps and required human checks.

## Next action
The first concrete step, the procedure/rules to read and any active process or shared-resource ownership that must be respected. Existing authorization and pending approvals remain explicit.
