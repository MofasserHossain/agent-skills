# Context policy

Load the current skill, applicable project instructions and the selected work record first. Follow source/spec/research pointers when relevant. Do not preload every skill, every ticket or all diffs.

Continue a session when its reasoning is useful to the next phase and context remains adequate. Start fresh for independent tasks with complete records. Handoff when changing tools, folders or people. Compact at a phase boundary when useful context must be retained.

A handoff states the objective, agreed decisions, unresolved questions, current revision, next action and pointers to artifacts. Do not duplicate entire artifacts or credentials.

Preserve the reason for consequential decisions, not just their conclusions. Research notes record sources, date, findings and limitations. An easily repeated configuration lookup rarely needs a permanent copy.

Keep stable instruction prefixes reusable and task context narrow. Measure quality and total successful-task cost; do not shorten context by removing essential constraints.

When selecting a model or reasoning effort, use [model policy](model-policy.md); native settings remain the executor's responsibility.
